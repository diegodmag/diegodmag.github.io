ABOUT


Live, Play and Tell stories.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) and have professional experience developing VR, web, and PC projects with Unity. Additionally, I am a Teaching Assistant at the Science Faculty in subjects such as Computer Graphics and Virtual Reality. I do my best to share what I know and to learn from my students.

I enjoy building decoupled, maintainable systems using software design patterns, as well as the process of game design and testing. I am especially interested in roles and graduate programs that combine game programming with design.
